Privacy Policy: YouTube Auto Uploader

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses this tool

YouTube Auto Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel, for that channel. It has no other users and does not offer sign-in to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube (the owner's own channel): upload videos as Private, add them to the owner's existing playlists, and read the owner's recent uploads and playlists to avoid duplicates.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets: only the specific folder and spreadsheet the owner has shared with the tool, to find finished videos and read their titles and descriptions.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel and to record the result (such as the YouTube video ID) in the owner's spreadsheet and in the owner's private records. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Short failure alerts (an episode code and a brief reason, with no links, IDs or passwords) may be sent to the owner's own phone.

Storage and security

Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub repository settings and are never shown in logs. Video files are copied temporarily to process the upload and deleted immediately afterwards. Upload records contain IDs only.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
