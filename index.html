YouTube Auto Uploader

A private tool for one YouTube channel

YouTube Auto Uploader is a small personal automation used only by the owner of a single YouTube channel. It uploads the owner's own finished videos from the owner's Google Drive to the owner's YouTube channel, always as Private, and adds them to the owner's existing playlists.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
